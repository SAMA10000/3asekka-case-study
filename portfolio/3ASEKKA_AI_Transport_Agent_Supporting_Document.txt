3ASEKKA AI TRANSPORT AGENT
Agents at Work 2026 – Supporting Document

1. PROJECT
Name: 3ASEKKA AI Transport Agent
Product: 3ASEKKA (عالسكة) – an on-demand goods transport platform.
Public GitHub: https://github.com/SAMA10000/3asekka-case-study
Hackathon project folder: hackathon-agent/

2. THE PROBLEM
Many small businesses, shops, and merchants in Egypt still arrange local goods transport through phone calls and informal driver networks.
This can make the process slow and inconsistent:
- The customer must explain the load manually.
- The correct vehicle may be unclear.
- Pricing may be difficult to estimate.
- Driver offers are difficult to compare.
- Important details can be missed during calls.

3. THE SOLUTION
3ASEKKA AI Transport Agent allows the customer to describe a transport request in natural Arabic, either by text or voice.
Example:
"عايز أنقل 20 كرتونة من سموحة للمنشية بكرة الساعة 3، وزنهم حوالي 250 كيلو."

The agent can:
- Understand Egyptian Arabic.
- Accept voice or text input.
- Extract pickup and destination.
- Understand cargo type and approximate weight.
- Ask only for missing information.
- Recommend a suitable vehicle.
- Estimate the transport fare.
- Prepare the transport request.
- Move the customer into the driver-bidding workflow.
- Display driver offers.
- Allow the customer to choose an offer.

4. HOW THE AI AGENT WORKS
1) Customer types or speaks a natural Arabic transport request.
2) Gemini handles language understanding and extracts the transport details.
3) The system validates required information and does not allow the AI to invent critical transport data.
4) Deterministic transport rules recommend a suitable vehicle.
5) The route/pricing stage estimates the fare when distance is available.
6) The request becomes a structured transport job.
7) Suitable driver offers appear in the bidding workflow.
8) The customer compares offers and selects a driver.

5. WHERE THIS FITS INSIDE THE 3ASEKKA APP
The AI Agent is not a separate application. It is designed as an easier way to create a transport request inside the existing 3ASEKKA customer app.

Customer Home
→ "اطلب بالكلام أو الصوت"
→ Customer speaks or types the transport request
→ AI fills the transport details automatically
→ Customer confirms the request
→ Waiting for Driver Offers
→ Driver Bidding
→ Customer selects a driver
→ Existing 3ASEKKA trip workflow continues

6. WHY IT IS MORE THAN A CHATBOT
The agent converts natural language into transport operations and decides which workflow step should happen next.
Agent actions include:
- extract_transport_request
- request_missing_information
- recommend_vehicle
- calculate_route
- estimate_fare
- prepare_transport_request
- driver_matching_and_bidding

7. TECHNOLOGY
AI: Google Gemini
Backend: Node.js
Agent logic: Custom transport tools and deterministic business rules
Voice: Egyptian Arabic browser speech recognition
Workflow: Vehicle recommendation, fare estimation, request preparation, and driver bidding
Existing product integration: 3ASEKKA transport workflow

8. SAFETY AND RELIABILITY
The public hackathon repository does not expose:
- Production Supabase secrets
- Private user or driver data
- Signing keys
- Production database credentials
- Private production backend logic

If the AI provider is unavailable, the demo can fall back to deterministic parsing rather than failing completely.

10. HACKATHON VALUE
The project focuses on a real operational problem for Egyptian businesses: making local goods transport easier to request, understand, price, and coordinate.
The AI layer is designed to work on top of an existing transport product rather than as a standalone chatbot.
